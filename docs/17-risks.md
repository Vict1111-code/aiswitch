# Risks and metrics

Status: proposed baseline — 2026-11-25.

Critical: credential exposure, tenant leakage, sandbox escape. High: credit expiry, incompatible APIs, fabricated citations, uncontrolled spending, scope creep. Mitigate with vault, isolation, adapter contracts, evidence verification, caps and phased releases. Targets: zero unauthorized writes, 95% citation validity, 100% billable requests recorded, 60% pilot activation and 35% week-four retention (hypotheses).
